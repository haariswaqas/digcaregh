# DigCare User Roles — What Every Role Can Do

> **digcaregh.com · For Healthcare Providers & Patients**
>
> DigCare is Ghana's all-in-one digital healthcare platform — connecting patients, doctors, clinics, pharmacies, labs, and insurers in one seamless mobile experience. Every person in the healthcare chain has their own dedicated role, their own personalised dashboard, and their own set of tools — built to make healthcare in Ghana simpler, faster, and more human.

---

## How DigCare Roles Work

When you sign up for DigCare, you choose a role that matches who you are in the healthcare system. Your role determines what you see when you open the app — your home screen, your quick actions, and the features available to you. You only ever see what is relevant to you.

There are **three main role categories**:

| Category | Roles |
|---|---|
| 🏥 **Patients** | Adult Patient · Student Patient · Visitor Patient |
| 🩺 **Medical Professionals** | Doctor · Pharmacist · Lab Technician |
| 🏢 **Facility Administrators** | Clinic Admin · Pharmacy Admin · Lab Admin |
| 🛡️ **Insurance Providers** | Insurance Provider |

## 🏥 Patients

Patients are at the heart of DigCare. Whether you are an adult managing your long-term health, a student at a campus clinic, or a visitor seeking care away from home, DigCare gives you a complete, private digital health record — on your phone.

All patient types share the same powerful core experience, with personalised greetings and accent colours that feel at home for each type of patient.

---

### Patient — Core Dashboard

Every patient's home screen shows:

- **Your profile avatar** and name — tap to go directly to your profile page.
- **A live notification badge** — so you never miss a message from your doctor, a new bill, or a health update.
- **Your DigCare Health Card** (mini view) — your unique digital health identity. Tap it to expand the full card, with your card number, type, QR code, and expiry. Doctors scan your QR code to access your records instantly — with your approval.
- **Your Health Summary** — three live counters showing your total Appointments, Prescriptions, and Lab Orders. Each counter is a shortcut that takes you directly to that section.
- **Upcoming Appointments** — a horizontal scroll of your next scheduled visits. Each card shows the doctor's name, specialty, date, time, and appointment type. Online teleconsult cards show an "Encrypted Room · Check-in Open" indicator. Tap a card to join a teleconsult or view full appointment details.
- **Quick Actions** — a grid of fast-track shortcuts to the most important features for your role.
- **Insurance Coverage** — a live card showing whether you have active, verified insurance, or a prompt to add insurance if you don't.
- **Recent Activity** — a timestamped feed of your latest bills, appointments, prescriptions, and lab orders, with one-tap access to each item.

### What All Patients Can Access

Beyond the quick-action grid, every patient can access the following features from within the app:

#### 🩺 Appointments
- Book in-person or online (teleconsult) appointments with doctors at registered DigCare facilities.
- View your full appointment history and upcoming schedule.
- Join video consultations directly from the app — no third-party app needed.
- Track the status of each appointment: Pending, Confirmed, Completed, or Cancelled.

#### 💊 Prescriptions
- View all prescriptions issued to you by doctors.
- See which prescriptions have been dispensed by a pharmacist and which are still pending.
- Track prescription status — issued, pending collection, or dispensed.

#### 🔬 Lab Orders
- View lab test orders placed for you by a doctor.
- Track the status of each test — ordered, in-progress, or results available.

#### 🏦 Billing & Payments
- View itemised bills for consultations, prescriptions, and lab tests.
- Pay using Mobile Money (MoMo), card, or bank transfer — all powered by Paystack.
- Your card numbers and Mobile Money PINs are **never stored** by DigCare.
- Emergency MoMo billing is enabled once your profile is complete.

#### 🛡️ Insurance
- Link your insurance plan to your DigCare account.
- See whether your insurance is active and verified in real time.
- View coverage details including your insurance provider name, plan status, and days until expiry.
- Add insurance to get covered for primary healthcare and teleconsultations.

#### 🪪 DigCare Health Card
- Your unique digital health identity — always with you, always secure.
- Contains your card number, card type (e.g. standard, premium), QR code, and expiry date.
- Doctors scan your QR code to pull up your medical records instantly — but only after you grant approval.
- Full card view available at any time from your home screen.

#### 🔐 Health Record Access Control
- See a complete log of every healthcare provider who has requested or accessed your records.
- Approve or deny remote access requests from doctors or specialists.
- Your data, your rules — every access event is logged and visible to you.

#### 💬 In-App Messaging (Chat)
- Secure, private messaging with your doctors.
- Continue conversations after appointments, ask follow-up questions, or request prescription refills.

#### 🔔 Notifications
- Real-time push notifications and in-app alerts for appointment confirmations, new messages, bill updates, and more.
- Unread count displayed as a badge on your notification icon.

#### 👤 Profile
- Add and update your personal information — first name, last name, profile picture, phone number, and address.
- Completing your profile unlocks emergency MoMo billing and e-prescriptions.

### Doctor

Doctors are the primary clinical users of DigCare. The doctor dashboard is themed in **deep navy blue** (`#1E3A8A`), signifying professionalism and clinical clarity.

#### Dashboard Overview

A doctor's home screen shows:

- **Profile info card** — name, specialty, online/offline status, current time, location (reverse-geocoded from GPS), username, and phone number.
- **Stats Summary** — live counters for total Appointments, Prescriptions, and Lab Orders. Each is a direct link to the relevant section.
- **Upcoming Appointments** — a horizontal scroll of the next scheduled patient visits. Each card shows the patient's name, appointment type, date, time, and status (Confirmed or Pending). Tap any card for full details.
- **Upcoming Shifts** — a horizontal scroll of the doctor's next scheduled clinic shifts, showing the clinic name, day, and shift hours.
- **Quick Actions** — a full grid of clinical tools (see below).
- **Recent Activity** — a live feed of the doctor's latest appointments, prescriptions issued, and lab orders, with timestamps and status badges.

#### Quick Actions

| Action | What It Does |
|---|---|
| 📋 **Prescriptions** | View all prescriptions you have issued. Filter by status, search by patient name, and track dispensing. |
| ➕ **Issue Prescription** | Write and send a new digital prescription to a patient's account. The prescription is automatically visible to the patient and any linked pharmacy. |
| 🔬 **Order Labs** | Submit a new lab test order for a patient. The order goes directly to the lab and is visible to the patient. |
| 📅 **My Shifts** | View your current and upcoming work shifts across all facilities you are registered with. |
| 🧪 **Lab Orders** | See all lab orders you have placed, with status tracking (ordered, processing, results ready). |
| 📆 **Appointments** | Full appointment management — view all upcoming and past consultations, sorted by date and status. |
| 📹 **Video Calls** | Access your video consultation hub — join or start a teleconsult session with a patient. |
| 💬 **Messages** | Open your inbox — read and reply to patient messages and colleague communications. |
| 📲 **Scan Health Card** | Open the QR scanner to read a patient's DigCare Health Card and pull up their medical records. Access is logged and requires patient consent. |
| 🏢 **Scan Facility Card** | Scan a facility's QR card to verify and log into a clinic's system digitally. |
| 🔑 **Access Requests** | Manage pending remote access requests from other providers who want to view a patient's records. Approve or deny each request. |

#### What Doctors Can Also Do

- **View patient appointment history** — full consultation notes and appointment metadata.
- **Track prescription status** — know when a pharmacy has dispensed a prescription you wrote.
- **Teleconsult** — run live video consultations with patients. View a patient's history, note symptoms, make a diagnosis, plan treatment, prescribe medicine, order lab tests, and make referrals — all within a single consultation session.
- **Shift management** — view which clinics have added them to the schedule and when their next shifts start.
- **In-app messaging** — communicate privately with patients before, during, and after consultations.

## Registration & Verification

### How to Join DigCare

1. **Download the app** — available on iOS and Android. Works with your existing NHIS number.
2. **Choose your role** — Patient, Doctor, Pharmacist, Lab Technician, Facility Admin, or Insurance Provider.
3. **Complete registration** — fill in your details, verify your phone number via OTP, and set a secure password.
4. **Verification (for professionals and facilities)** — medical professionals and facility administrators go through a manual verification step. The DigCare team reviews government-issued IDs, professional licences, and facility documentation before activating the account.
5. **Access your dashboard** — once verified, you land directly on your personalised home screen.

### Why Verification Matters

We verify every healthcare provider on DigCare — because your health depends on knowing who you are dealing with. Doctors are checked against their professional licences. Facilities are verified with their registration documents. This is not optional — it is how we keep DigCare safe for everyone.

---

## A Note on Patient Privacy

Your health records on DigCare are yours. Here is how we protect them:

- **Consent-based access** — a doctor can only view your records if you approve their access request. Every approval and denial is logged.
- **QR-gated entry** — in a physical setting, a doctor scans your health card QR code. That scan creates an access log entry immediately.
- **Full access history** — you can see exactly who accessed your records, when, and from which facility.
- **PIN and OTP verification** — sensitive actions require additional identity confirmation.
- **No stored payment secrets** — your card numbers and Mobile Money PINs are never stored by DigCare. All payment processing goes through Paystack, a trusted and certified payment provider.

## Accessibility Requirements

- All images must have descriptive `alt` text (not repeated across screenshots).
- All interactive elements must be keyboard-navigable.
- Colour contrast ratio must be ≥ 4.5:1 for body text, ≥ 3:1 for large text against backgrounds.
- Tab switching (facility admin page) must use `role="tablist"`, `role="tab"`, `aria-selected` attributes.
- Form labels in the contact and waitlist forms must be explicitly linked to inputs via `for`/`id`.
- Animated orbs must respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` — disable or pause animations when the user has requested reduced motion.
